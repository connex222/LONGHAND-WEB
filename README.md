# Longhand

A seven-letter word deduction puzzle. One a day.

This is the web version. Longhand is also published as an Android app.

**Open it:** https://connex222.github.io/LONGHAND-WEB/

## How to use it

1. Open the link above in Chrome, Edge or Safari, on a phone, tablet or computer.
2. On a phone, use the browser's **Add to Home Screen** option to keep it one tap away.

Or download `index.html` and open it straight from your device. Everything is in that one file.

## Saving

Your streak and progress are saved on the device you play on. Your data is not sent anywhere. Clearing the browser's data deletes it.
